Piano samples used by HarmonyPlug
==================================

"Salamander Grand Piano V3" by Alexander Holm (Yamaha C5 grand piano),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported license (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Original: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
Source of these MP3 renders: https://tambien.github.io/Piano/audio/ (the @tonejs/piano sample set,
the Salamander recordings converted to MP3).

Changes made for HarmonyPlug: 30 notes (every minor third, A0 to C8), velocity layers 5 and 13
of 16, trimmed to 3.5-8 s with a 1.2 s fade-out and re-encoded to 112 kbps MP3. The app
pitch-shifts between neighbouring samples.
